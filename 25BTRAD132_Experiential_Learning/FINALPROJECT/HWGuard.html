🚀 1-Click Launchers on this Pendrive

To perform deep kernel-level SMBIOS, RAM slot serial, and physical NVMe/SATA disk audits, open the corresponding launcher for your machine:

🪟 Windows (10 / 11)
Double-click HWGuard.bat on the pendrive root. It launches the native Windows SMBIOS engine without console clutter.
H:\HWGuard.bat
🍎 macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel)
Double-click HWGuard.command on the pendrive root. Finder executes the Mac hardware profiler in Terminal.
./HWGuard.command
🐧 Linux (Ubuntu, Fedora, Arch)
Execute HWGuard.sh from desktop or terminal. It reads /sys/class/dmi/id and launches the dashboard.
./HWGuard.sh
📱 Android (USB-C OTG)
Plug via USB-C OTG, tap HWGuard.html in Samsung My Files or Google Files. Runs 100% offline in Chrome!
Open HWGuard.html
